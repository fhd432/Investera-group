Our journey so far

A partnership that grew from a single asset class into a coordinated, multi-market platform.

2012 – 2013

Idea generation and pooling initiated

The idea of a collective, multi-asset investment vehicle takes shape among the founding partners, and pooling of capital begins.

2014 – 2016

Slow pace period

A deliberately quiet phase, with limited activity while the model was tested.

2017 – 2018

Rebuilding and reorganising

The group is reorganized and capital allocation practices are strengthened.

2019

Restructuring & capital markets

InvestEra restructures and introduces a dedicated capital market investment strategy.

2023

Five blocks, one portfolio

A new strategy is put in place around the "five blocks, one portfolio" theme, alongside formation of the executive committee.

2024

First Private Equity investment

InvestEra takes its first direct stake in an operating business.

2025

1 million landmark

The portfolio crosses the 1 million landmark.

2026

Private Equity outperforms

Private Equity investment outperforms the Capital Market portfolio for the first time.
